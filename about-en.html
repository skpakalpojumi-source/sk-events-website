ABOUT US

Events where everything works together.

SK Events provides DJ, photo, video and technical solutions for events in Latvia and Scandinavia. From small private celebrations to weddings, corporate events and conferences, we combine experience, quality equipment and a thoughtful approach in one team.

Our goal is simple: the client should not have to worry about technical details, and the event should look, sound and be remembered exactly as planned.

We care about the atmosphere - light, sound, rhythm and the feeling that brings guests together in one event.

“Atmosphere begins when everything in the event starts working together.”
