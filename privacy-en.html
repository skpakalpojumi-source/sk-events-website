Privacy policy

Your data is used only for communication about your request.

This policy explains how SK Events processes information submitted through website contact forms or sent to us directly.

What information we process

When you complete a request form, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, event date, location, guest count and any other information you choose to provide.

Why the data is needed

We use the information to contact you, clarify event details, prepare a suitable offer and provide the requested service.

Data storage and sharing

We keep the data for as long as necessary for communication and cooperation. We do not share information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your rights

You may ask us to correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data by contacting us at info@skevents.eu.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@skevents.eu or call 26252236.
